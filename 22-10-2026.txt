What is the Linux File System?

. A Linux file system is the way Linux organizes and stores files and directories on a computer or server.

. In Linux, the root directory / is the starting point of the entire file system.

. /bin Essential user commands, often linked to /usr/bin

. /sbin System administration commands, often linked to /usr/sbin

. /boot Files needed to start Linux

. /dev Device files representing hardware and virtual devices

. /proc Virtual files containing process and kernel information

. /tmp Temporary files

. /var Frequently changing data, such as logs

. /usr Many applications, commands, and shared files

. /opt Optional or third-party software

. /mnt Commonly used as a temporary mount point

. /media Commonly used for removable devices
